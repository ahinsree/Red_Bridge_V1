# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Corporate executives, business leaders, and enterprise partners seeking strategic advisory, custom digital solutions, and high-impact advisory briefings.

## Product Purpose

Provide the corporate digital presence and interactive advisory portal for Red Bridge Advisory, delivering thought leadership (Insights), service capabilities, digital business tools, and interactive client engagement.

## Positioning

Boutique high-touch executive consulting with custom digital portals, combining radical integration, scientific precision, and delivery sprint standards.

## Operating Context

- Corporate digital portal and interactive advisory platform.
- Git-based CMS operations (Decap CMS) for non-technical content editors.
- Interactive briefing slide-drawers and 3D WebGL visualizations.
- Digital visiting card routes (`/sarath-v-raj`, `/jishnu-g-anand`, `/ahinsree-b`) with scannable vCard support.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Stack:** Next.js 15, React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion 12, Three.js (WebGL 3D).
- **CMS & Operations:** Decap CMS for Insights articles with automated GitHub Actions build and deployment.
- **Interactive Features:** Floating AI Chatbot assistant with rule-based response mapping, slide-in briefing drawers, lead submission proxy via Google Sheets API.
- **Constraints:** GitHub Pages static site deployment, clean build and linting (`npm run lint`), strict type-checking (`tsc --noEmit`).

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** Red Bridge Advisory (RBA).
- **Visual Identity:** Dark theme glassmorphism, crimson/red accents (`#B22030`), high-precision typography, dynamic 3D elements, and sleek micro-animations.

## Evidence on Hand

- Corporate narrative and advisory methodology documented in `README.md`.
- Content management schema and markdown articles in `src/data/insights/`.
- Digital visiting cards and vCards in `public/` and `src/app/`.

## Product Principles

1. **Executive Precision:** Every interaction must feel high-touch, polished, and tailored for executive decision-makers.
2. **Scientific & Radical Integration:** Combine deep strategic research with dynamic, modern web capabilities.
3. **Seamless Digital Operations:** Keep content authoring accessible via Git CMS without compromising performance.
4. **Interactive Engagement:** Use 3D elements, drawers, and responsive UI elements to make advisory concepts tangible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA compliance for web readability, keyboard navigation, structured semantics, and responsive tap targets.
